Hi, I'm Lawrence. I work on problems in graph theory and mathematical physics.

I do research in Ramsey and spectral graph theory with a professor at UCSD, and photocurrent microscopy at a UCLA lab. I also run Mission Mitosis, a 501(c)(3) bringing hands-on science back to schools in the Beaverton area.
