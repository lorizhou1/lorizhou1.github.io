Hello there, I'm Lawrence! I'm broadly interested in the intersection of solid state physics and intelligent computing. I specifically am interested in using quantum properties of matter to achieve intelligent computing, along with the discovery and categorization of such materials.

I also do some side projects in combinatorics (specifically Ramsey theory), and number theory.
