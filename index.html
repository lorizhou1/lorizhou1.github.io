I'm Lawrence. I'm broadly interested in the intersection of solid state physics and intelligent computing, specifically in using quantum properties of matter to achieve intelligent computing. I currently am working under a professor at UCLA.

I also do extensive work combinatorics (specifically Ramsey theory), and number theory (semirings, Galois theory). If I am not doing these then I'm probably reading or going on hikes.

papers
  • 2026

    Improved Explicit Constructions for R(5, t)

    Explicit and algorithmic graph constructions giving a new lower bound for off-diagonal Ramsey number r(5,t). This improves the results of Kostochka, Pudlák, and Rödl (2010).

    arXiv Link

  • 2025-2026

    Analogues of the Goldbach Conjecture

    The Goldbach conjecture extended to Laurent series semidomains. This was done as a part of CrowdMath 2025, under Dr. Harold Polo, Dr. Felix Gotti, and Dr. Marly Gotti.

    arXiv preprint soon

  • 2026

    Fast Spectral Certification of Triangle-Free Graphs

    Applications of spectral graph theory in certifying Ramsey bounds faster using Fourier block decomposition. Method demonstrated through a family of connected Petersen graphs.

  • 2025

    Turán Density of k-Uniform Hypergraphs

    Expository work on extremal properties of 3-uniform hypergraphs, studying co-degree distributions and Turán-type densities, and investigated constructions with high minimum co-degree while avoiding tetrahedra.

  • 2024

    Modeling of Heat Transfer Media and Optimization

    Modeled the Navier-Stokes equations for incompressible fluid flow and the effect of heat loss in a concentrating solar power system, determined optimal salt given constraints.
